AI in OpsOwl — How It Helps + Responsible Use
AI that is useful and auditable
Three ways AI accelerates investigation. Five principles that keep it responsible.
HOW AI HELPS
🤖
AI Investigation Copilot
Answers investigative questions using uploaded case documents. "Does this account show prior structuring patterns?" — it reads the documents, not training data. Every answer cites its source. Streams results in real time.
✍️
AI Narrative Generator
Drafts a complete PMLA-compliant STR narrative in 90 seconds — grounded exclusively in the approved findings on the case. Never reads training data. Never invents facts. Every sentence links to source evidence.
🔍
Document Risk Analysis (AI)
Analyzes uploaded KYC, bank statements, and transaction histories. Flags anomalies, patterns, and typology indicators in 45 seconds. Saves the analyst 30–45 minutes of manual document review per case.
HOW WE KEEP IT RESPONSIBLE
1
AI reads case facts. Not training data.
The STR narrative AI reads only the approved findings on the specific case. It has no access to the internet, no access to external data, and does not use training data to fill gaps. INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT is returned when evidence is missing.
2
Every AI output requires human approval. Always.
All AI outputs start as "pending." A named second reviewer must approve before the case advances. This is enforced in Go backend code — it cannot be bypassed with a toggle or a shortcut.
3
PAN, Aadhaar, mobile — masked before reaching the model.
Microsoft Presidio strips all Indian PII identifiers before any text is sent to the AI model. Tokens ([PAN_1], [AADHAAR_1]) are used instead. The real values never reach the model or its logs.
4
Every AI decision is logged permanently.
Model version, full prompt, output, confidence score, reviewer name, reviewer timestamp — stored permanently, never purged. FIU-IND can ask "who approved this AI output?" and the answer is one query.
5
AI is read-only. It cannot take any action.
The AI investigation agent has zero write-capable tools. It cannot create, update, close, or file anything. Every action in the system is taken by a named human, with a logged timestamp.

Business Terms — Know These Before You Walk In
These are terms the Decentro compliance team will use naturally. Use them back — it signals you understand their world.
STR — Suspicious Transaction Report
The formal report filed with FIU-IND when a regulated entity suspects money laundering. Required by law under PMLA. Missing one is a criminal offence. The entire OpsOwl workflow leads to this output.
"OpsOwl reduces alert-to-STR time from 10–15 days to under 20 minutes."
FIU-IND — Financial Intelligence Unit India
The government body under the Ministry of Finance that receives all STR filings and conducts compliance examinations. Think of it as the primary AML regulator for financial institutions in India.
"When FIU-IND walks in for an examination, you open OpsOwl — not a shared drive."
PMLA — Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002
India's primary AML law. Mandates record-keeping for 5 years (Section 12), STR reporting to FIU-IND, and internal controls. All OpsOwl records meet PMLA Section 12 requirements permanently.
"OpsOwl meets PMLA Section 12 record-keeping out of the box."
TMS — Transaction Monitoring System
Software that watches every transaction and fires alerts on suspicious patterns. OpsOwl does NOT replace TMS — it receives alerts FROM TMS via webhook and manages the investigation from there.
"We connect to your TMS in one business day — no changes to your TMS setup."
goAML
The UNODC-built software platform used by FIU-IND to receive STR filings. STRs must be submitted in goAML 4.0 XML format. OpsOwl generates this XML automatically in one click — correct format, all required fields.
"One-click goAML 4.0 XML. FIU-IND ready. No formatting errors."
FINnet — Financial Intelligence Network
The secure network through which STRs are submitted to FIU-IND. Think of it as the delivery pipe — goAML is the format, FINnet is how it gets there. OpsOwl produces the file; submission goes through FINnet.
Say "we export directly to FINnet via goAML 4.0 XML."
4-Eye Principle / Maker-Checker
Two separate people must review before anything is finalised. Maker creates, Checker approves. In OpsOwl, this is enforced in Go backend code — it cannot be bypassed by any toggle, shortcut, or user permission.
"The analyst who drafts the STR cannot approve it. Second reviewer enforced in code."
FATF Typology Codes
FATF (Financial Action Task Force) is the global AML standards body. Typology codes classify the pattern of suspicious behaviour — e.g., Structuring (ST-01), Layering (LA-03), Smurfing. OpsOwl requires a typology code on every finding before the narrative can be generated.
"Structuring + Layering" is what Arjun Mehta Enterprises was doing in the demo case — ST-01 and LA-03.
Alert Triage
The process of reviewing TMS alerts and deciding: is this worth investigating, or is it a false positive? Most TMS alerts (80–95%) are false positives. OpsOwl provides a risk-ranked queue and bulk-dismiss capability to handle this efficiently.
"Analysts spend less time triaging, more time on cases that matter."
Investigation Playbook
A pre-defined checklist that auto-assigns when a case is created by case type. Ensures every analyst follows the same steps: collect evidence, document findings, get 4-eye approval, draft narrative, export goAML. Cases cannot close until all playbook tasks are complete.
"Every analyst follows the same process. New hires investigate like veterans from day one."
Hash-Chain Audit Trail
Each database record contains a cryptographic hash of the previous record. If any past record is modified, the chain breaks — tampering is detectable. OpsOwl applies this to every action on every case. Proves to a regulator that the investigation record was not altered after the fact.
"The audit trail is hash-sealed — any tampering breaks the chain detectably."
On-Premise (On-Prem)
Software running entirely on the customer's own servers — not on the vendor's cloud. For compliance teams, on-prem means sensitive investigation data (KYC, transaction records, subject PII) never leaves the organization's controlled environment.
"On-prem means zero data egress. Your IT security team approves this in one meeting."
Air-Gap Deployment
Server with zero internet connectivity — completely isolated network. OpsOwl ships an air-gap bundle — a tarball with every dependency pre-packaged — so it can run in a completely isolated environment with no inbound or outbound internet.
"We have an air-gap bundle. Your IT team never needs to open a port."
Obligated Entity
Any business legally required to comply with PMLA — banks, NBFCs, fintechs, payment aggregators, insurance companies. Decentro is an obligated entity as a payment aggregator regulated by RBI. Non-compliance can result in penalties or criminal prosecution.
Use this term to show you understand Decentro's regulatory position.
PEP — Politically Exposed Person
High-risk customer category — politicians, government officials, their family members. Regulated entities must apply enhanced due diligence. OpsOwl flags PEP status on subject profiles and adds extra approval requirements on PEP-related cases.
"PEP screening is built in — it flags on subject create, not just at onboarding."
Webhook Integration
A simple HTTP POST from your TMS to OpsOwl whenever a new alert fires. OpsOwl validates the HMAC signature and creates a case automatically. One-time setup: share one sample alert, we configure field mapping, webhook live in 1 business day.
"Webhook goes live in 1 business day. Zero changes to your TMS."
STR Narrative
The written description in an STR: who the subject is, what they did, why it's suspicious, and what the analyst found. Most time-consuming part of STR filing — 60–90 minutes per case. OpsOwl's AI drafts this in 90 seconds from approved findings.
"90 seconds to draft what currently takes an analyst an hour to write from scratch."
Structuring
A common money laundering typology where a person breaks large transactions into smaller ones to avoid reporting thresholds (e.g., multiple transfers below ₹2L). FATF Typology ST-01. This is exactly what Rajesh Kumar Gupta was doing in the demo case.
Use this word — compliance teams know it and it immediately contextualises the demo case.
